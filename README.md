# 👩🏻‍💻 Deborah Pongeluppe

**`Data Analyst`**

My name is Deborah Pongeluppe. I am Italian-Brazilian and currently live in Belgium. I hold a Law degree from the Pontifical Catholic University of Minas Gerais and, driven by curiosity about how decisions are made in the digital world, I specialized in Data Analysis. This combination allows me to approach technology problems through two complementary lenses: the legal one, which brings structure, ethics, and risk assessment; and the analytical one, which transforms data into testable hypotheses, metrics, and actions.
